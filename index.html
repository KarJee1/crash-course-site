Common questions

Is this real money? No. Everything in the app is simulated with virtual money. You can't deposit, withdraw or trade real securities.

Are the stock prices real? Stock and ETF prices come from a statistical market simulation. They use real company names, but they are not real quotes. Crypto prices are real, live prices from Coinbase's public market data, and those trades are still simulated.

How do I move my portfolio to a new device? Sign in to the same Apple ID with iCloud turned on. Your portfolio and watchlist sync automatically.

How do I start over? Go to Settings in the app and choose Reset portfolio. You can pick a new starting amount.

Is this financial advice? No. Crash Course is an educational simulator. Nothing in it is a recommendation to buy or sell any security.
