Privacy Policy

Stock Market: Crash Course · Effective October 29, 2026

Crash Course is a market simulator for practicing investing with virtual money. It is built so that we never see your data.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We don't run any servers that receive information about you.

What stays on your device

Your simulated portfolio, orders, watchlist, settings and Replay progress are stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on, your portfolio and watchlist are also stored in your own iCloud account through Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so they sync across your devices and survive a reinstall. Apple handles that storage under Apple's Privacy Policy. We can't access it.

Live crypto prices

To show live crypto prices, the app requests public market data directly from Coinbase's public API. These requests contain no personal information, but like any internet request they reveal your device's IP address to Coinbase. See Coinbase's Privacy Policy. Stock and ETF prices are simulated on your device and need no network access.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. If the app ever starts collecting data, for example to show ads, we'll say so here and in the App Store listing before that version is released.
